Timing Belt Design & Mounting Notes
Tooth Count & Center Distance: Tooth count (zb) is rounded to the nearest integer. Check manufacturer catalogs for off-the-shelf closed loops, and provide slot adjustments (-1.5% to +2.5%) on motor mounts to accommodate standard lengths.
Teeth In Mesh Criterion: Industry handbooks require at least 6 teeth fully engaged on the small pulley (z_mesh ≥ 6) to deliver nominal rated torque without tooth shear.
